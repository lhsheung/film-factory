几多筒菲林 (JiDuo Tong FeiLin) — third-party notices
=====================================================

This application is an independent Windows front end for the open-source
SpektraLab / spektrafilm film and print model. It does not bundle the macOS
application or the film profiles. When you connect a locally built Windows
engine, that engine and the profiles it loads remain under their own licences.
