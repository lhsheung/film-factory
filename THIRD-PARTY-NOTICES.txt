菲林工厂 is an independent Windows front end and port for the open-source
SpektraLab / spektrafilm film and print model. It ships two things that come
from upstream:

  * assets/profiles — the film and paper profiles (CC BY-SA 4.0, see below),
    redistributed unmodified so the app can evaluate their measured curves;
  * engine/ — a Windows binary built from the SpektraLab v1.3.3 sources
    (GPL-3.0-or-later), placed next to the executable by scripts/package.js.

https://github.com/JamesQiu2005/SpektraLab
